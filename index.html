Nilufer Tamatgar

AI Researcher

As an AI Researcher & Engineer, I worked at A*STAR (CFAR), specializing in deep generative models and machine learning for drug discovery. My research focused on advancing Transformer, Diffusion, and Graph architectures through supervised fine-tuning, preference optimization, and ranking objectives, specifically addressing the challenge of learning from sparse experimental measurements to maximize the precision of a model's top-ranked predictions. Evaluated on real-world experimental data, this work has been presented at premier AI Conferences.

Earlier in my career, I built a strong engineering foundation centered on full-stack application development, real-time machine learning model deployment, and physical-layer systems including IoT architectures, Wireless Sensor Networks, and Wideband Wireless Communications.
